# Third-party changes in Lewis 1.7

Fingerpose 0.1.0 (MIT), https://github.com/andypotato/fingerpose. Installed as a pinned production npm dependency; its licence is retained in node_modules and the lock file pins integrity. Recorded samples create curl descriptions, cross-checked against the local normalized landmark matcher.

Dafeiyu Pet (MIT), https://github.com/1190fasheqi/dafeiyu-pet, commit 5b0e01856116bd2bae82df1f43c32faa5f056196. Upstream files and licence are retained under vendor/dafeiyu-pet. pet-service/bridge.py adapts the Qt window: existing Lewis model/task routing over private process pipes, no independent Key storage, smaller sprites, alpha hit region, single Lewis tray, no upstream autostart or direct cloud calls from its chat/weather handlers. PySide6 is licensed under LGPLv3/GPL/commercial; its wheels retain licence texts in the local component environment and are not embedded in the Electron installer.

The investigated https://github.com/Lakshy-coder/gesture-trainer was not copied: no explicit licence was found, and its binary two-hand trainer is not a drop-in for Lewis's multi-template library.
